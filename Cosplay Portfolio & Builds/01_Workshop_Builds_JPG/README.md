## Overview

This portfolio documents high-end 3D printed, molded, sculpted, and painted cosplay armor, weapon props, and scenic pedestals created by **@cosplaydad3d**. The collection spans major gaming and fantasy franchises including **World of Warcraft (Lich King / Wrath of the Lich King)**, **Genshin Impact (Arlecchino / Crimson Moon's Semblance)**, **Fantasy & Elven Armory**, and **Primal Creature Skulls**.

## Technical Build Methods & Materials Summary
- **Fabrication**: Bambu Lab 3D Printing (FDM & Resin), tree supports, textured PEI plates.
- **Post-Processing**: Orbital sanding, automotive filler primer, gap filling.
- **Finishing & Painting**: Gloss black base coats, graphite powder / metallic drybrushing, acrylic washes, airbrushed ombre fades, protective clear coats.
- **Mixed Media**: Faux fur, paracord/jute bindings, leather strapping, translucent tinted resins/polymers.
